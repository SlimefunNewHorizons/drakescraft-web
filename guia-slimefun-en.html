🧪 Creative Lab & Cheat Mode
Safe Environment

The Laboratory (/warp laboratorio) is an isolated test environment for Slimefun. You can switch between creative and survival, but adventure or spectator modes are disabled. Strict boundaries protect the server and prevent free items from entering the survival economy.

/sf cheat

Opens the Slimefun Cheat Sheet, allowing you to withdraw machines, materials, and items. All researches appear unlocked while in this world, without modifying your external progression.Lab Only

Shift + Right Click with Guide

While holding the Slimefun Guide (/sf guide), sneak (Shift) and right-click to open the full cheat catalog directly without typing commands.Lab Only

Secure Isolation (InvSwitcher)

The gamemode engine backs up and isolates your inventory, ender chest, experience, health, hunger, statistics, and advancements. Remain still during the transfer and avoid alternate teleports.

Restricted Commands

/sf give and /sf research are administrative commands. Auctions, shops, player trade, virtual vaults, ender chests, and homes are blocked to prevent item smuggling. Use /sf cheat.

120-Block Limit per Chunk

There is a strict cap of 120 Slimefun blocks per chunk. High-risk explosives, explosive armaments, accelerators, and chunk loaders are restricted: visual effects render, but physical blast damage and server lag are neutralized.

🛡️ Golden Rules, Anti-Dupe & SRE Diagnostics
Critical
⚠️ CANONICAL ANVIL RULE: Never use Vanilla Minecraft anvils to combine or repair Slimefun tools, weapons, or armor. Vanilla anvils do not parse PersistentDataContainer (PDC) tags, stripping the internal slimefun:slimefun_item key and permanently destroying custom enchantments, energy buffers, and technological attributes. Always use the Slimefun Anvil or the InfinityExpansion Advanced Anvil.
Quantum Pusher Rule (Anti-Dupe)

Why can't you push items into a Quantum Storage unit using a Networks Pusher? In NetworkTransportUtils.java, Quantum Storage units are explicitly blacklisted as targets for external Pushers to prevent infinite item duplication exploits (#230/#240). The canonical network design is to connect the Quantum Storage directly via Network Cable (the network populates it automatically without needing a Pusher). If used in standalone mode, feed it with a Vanilla Hopper pointing directly into the top face (Slot 1).

Chunk Hibernation (SuiteTickerEngine)

Unlike legacy Slimefun which ran infinite Bukkit tasks for every machine consuming 100% CPU, SuiteTickerEngine indexes machines by active chunk. If no player is near the chunk, machines enter zero-CPU passive hibernation and instantly wake up when you return without losing tick progress.

Stalled Machines: 4-Step Diagnostic

1. Research: Open /sf guide and verify the item is unlocked.
2. Power Grid: Ensure the generator and machine are connected via cable and capacitor. Check your Joules buffer with SlimeHUD.
3. Output Slots: If the output inventory is full, the machine stalls defensively to prevent item loss.
4. Mob Cap: Electric Spawners pause automatically if 7 or more identical entities are clustered nearby.

Anti-Explosion & Protections

Machines and networks respect 100% of island protections (BentoBox) and territory claims. Unauthorized players cannot place pushers or extractors to siphon items from your protected chests.

⚔️ DrakesBosses vs Slimefun: The Martial Counterweight & Anti-AFK
Anti-Infinity Active
⚖️ THE MARTIAL COUNTERWEIGHT: On typical servers, endgame Slimefun degenerates into passive AFK farming behind invulnerable gear. On DrakesCraft, DrakesBosses was engineered specifically as the active martial counterweight to Slimefun technology.
Active Anti-Infinity Combat Logic

Combat engine classes FullInfinityArmorCounter.java and SlimefunArmorAdaptation.java actively inspect player loadouts for Infinity Singularity or high-tier SlimeTinker armor. During boss encounters against the 26 pantheonic deities, mathematical invulnerability is shattered: divine strikes pierce armor ratings as True Damage, preventing automated or passive victories.

26 Deities across 5 Pantheons

Greek: Zeus, Hades, Poseidon, Ares.
Norse: Thor, Odin, Kratos.
Egyptian: Ra, Anubis, Isis, Set.
Cataclysmic: Hydra, Wither Storm, Ancestral Dragon, Typhon.
Mythic & Sovereign: Cosmic Garou, Jax, Obsidian Sentinel, and the legendary Mahoraga featuring real-time kinetic adaptation.

Method 1: Physical Overworld Shrines

Construidos por jugadores con Pedestales sagrados (Crying Obsidian, Lodestone o Blackstone pulido). Ofrenda sagrada en mano: Lightning Rod para invocar a Zeus, Trident para Poseidón, Netherite Ingot para Ares o Wither Skull para Hades. Se desata una tormenta de rayos y pentagramas celestiales.

Método 2: Spawns Celestiales Naturales

Apariciones esporádicas en biomas salvajes de world, world_nether y world_the_end con alertas globales en el chat, relámpagos y títulos cinemáticos.

Método 3: Arenas del Olimpo (/bosswarp)

Domos de combate no destructivos con cobro de entrada en Dragmas vía Vault. Permite a los jugadores desafiar a los dioses en celdas seguras y cuenta con modo espectador interactivo (/bosswarp spectate <jugador>) y salida de emergencia (/bosswarp leave).

Fin de Progresión
La Fusión de Tres Mundos: El techo de cristal de Infinity se rompe en DrakesCraft. En lugar de que Slimefun y los Dioses operen como islas separadas, el Tier 10 Olympus Ascendancy une la energía de Suite 4, las aleaciones cuánticas de Suite 1 y los núcleos estelares divinos de Suite 6.
La Forja Primordial (Multibloque 7×7×5)

Estructura monumental de ingeniería construida con reactores de vacío, bloques cuánticos y pedestales de contención divina. Es la única forja del universo capaz de fundir materiales de Tier 10.

Ingredientes Tripartitos de la Convergencia

1. Energía Extrema: Requiere millones de Joules provenientes de Centrales Nucleares o Reactores de Vacío de DrakesGenerators (Suite 4).
2. Materia Cuántica: Singularidades puras forjadas en DrakesTech (Suite 1).
3. Núcleos Estelares Divinos: Esencias divinas que únicamente dropean los 26 dioses panteónicos al ser abatidos en combate cuerpo a cuerpo en DrakesBosses (Suite 6).

Equipo Primordial (Tier 10)

Herramientas y armaduras primordiales que trascienden el Infinity Singularity: daño cósmico irreducible, mitigación activa ante Daño Verdadero y afinidad elemental con el Olimpo.

Prestigio Cósmico (Paragón)

Al llegar al máximo de la tecnología, los jugadores pueden sacrificar un set completo de Infinity Singularity en el Altar de Dallas para ascender en el sistema de Paragón, desbloqueando cosméticos celestiales, títulos míticos y pasivas de cuenta permanentes.
