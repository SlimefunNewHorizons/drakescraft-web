Powers guide

How to get a power as a regular player, use it and grow it. All data comes from the datapack itself.

POWERS 16 obtainableMENU /trigger svm_ep.menu
CommandsThe ServerRanksSlimefun & AddonsPlugins & SuitesPowers

Start here

  1. Get Golden Flesh. Kill zombies (or husks, drowned and others) until one drops Golden Flesh. You don't need to be op.
  2. Eat it. It grants a random power and shows Obtained a Power! If you'd rather choose, craft a scroll.
  3. Open the menu. Type /trigger svm_ep.menu, go to Power Info and unlock abilities. You start with 10 points.
  4. Put Use Ability in your offhand. It is invisible on purpose. It appears in your inventory by itself.
  5. Pick a slot and hold right-click. Your hotbar slot (1 to 9) decides which ability fires. The action bar shows mana, ability and cooldown.

How to get a power

You only have one power at a time. The /loot give ... svm_ep:items/power_chooser command is admin-only; as a regular player there are two routes:

1. Golden Flesh (random power)

It's food, eaten normally. With no power, it assigns a random one of the 16 available. If you already have one, it gives 1 to 3 ability points.

Power mobs drop far more often: rolls of 15%, 30% and 60% depending on their strength, plus Golden Hearts. On this server at least 5% of zombies, husks, drowned, zombie villagers and zombified piglins spawn with a power.

Changing power

Craft a Power Reseter (8 redstone + 1 ender pearl, shapeless) and put it in your offhand. It removes your power so you can get another.

The “Use Ability” item

Why can't I see it in my hand? Its model has scale 0 in first and third person. That is on purpose, so it doesn't block your view or show in combat. You only see it in the inventory.
  • The server gives it automatically, checking every 7 seconds. If your inventory is full, it won't arrive.
  • It must be in a hand; without it your power is silenced. The item itself recommends the offhand (/offhand on Bedrock).
  • Holding right-click: internally it's a food that never finishes being eaten, so the game can detect you holding the button. Abilities marked “holding” or “charging” continue until you release, draining mana.
  • If nothing happens: the ability isn't unlocked, the slot is empty, or you're on cooldown (“On cooldown!”) or out of mana (“Not enough mana!”). If your main hand holds something usable, like food, Minecraft uses that first.

Binding abilities

Some come bound to your hotbar when you get a power. To change them: select the slot, open the menu, Power Info, the ability, then Bind. There are also sneak and sprint binds, and two hotbar sets.

In Settings you can switch activation to Sneak/Shift Click or stop receiving the item. Settings → Ability Inputs has a separate item (needs an empty main hand) to bind abilities to right-click, F, Q, jump and sprint, with or without sneaking, without switching slots.

Mana, points and level

SystemHow it works
ManaYou start with a 100 maximum and the server cap is 1,000. It rises by eating Golden Eyes (from spiders). It doesn't regenerate while using an ability. Calculated from the code at 20 TPS: about 14.8 per second when rested, down to 0.2 if you've overused it; that strain recovers with your hunger bar at 5 shanks or more. Golden Bone speeds up regeneration.
Ability pointsYou start with 10. You earn 10 per rewarding SVM Powers advancement, 1 to 3 per Golden Flesh if you already have a power, and 3 each time you reach a new level. They unlock and upgrade abilities.
Power levelRises by regenerating mana, meaning by using it. Evaluated every 40 seconds. The next level needs level × 6 + (level × 4 / 5)² + 100 progress: 100 at level 0, 224 at 10, 2,000 at 50. See it in Player & Stats.
Per-ability upgradesEach ability has two upgrades, cooldown reduction and cost reduction, up to 100 steps. Per the datapack they cost 1 point per step unless stated otherwise.

Cooldown and cost fall with level. Datapack formula: cooldown × 180 / (level + 180) and cost × 255 / (level + 255).

LevelCooldownCost

Dying doesn't take max mana or extra hearts under the current configuration. Golden Heart raises max health, with a configured cap of 20 on this server.

The 16 powers

Base cooldown at level 0; a range means it depends on how you use it. “—” means no fixed value in its use function. Copy learns abilities by copying them from mobs and power users; Flora unlocks its own by visiting climates. Snow, Invisibility and Blood exist in the datapack but are unfinished and can't be obtained.

This guide comes from reading the datapack itself (SVM Powers 1.86.1) and the Season 2 configuration, not from a test playthrough. Season 2 is still in testing, so these remain to be confirmed in-game:

  • Bedrock: whether the menu, hold-click and Use Ability work the same through Geyser.
  • Older Java: menus use dialogs, added to Minecraft in 1.21.6. Older clients may not show them.
  • Resource pack: the invisibility depends on the server pack; without it, Use Ability could show as a missing texture.
  • Costs: the table uses what the menu states; for some abilities the code charges a different number. Trust what you see in-game.
  • Level: the code also lets your level drop if you regenerate little mana; how noticeable that is remains to be measured.
  • Gamemodes: the datapack doesn't distinguish worlds. Which powers work in which gamemode in Season 2 is still to be confirmed.
