Code of Honor & Conduct

Official DrakesCraft Rules

Straightforward, fair and enforced equally for all players. Our goal is to maintain a healthy technical and survival multiplayer environment.

1 Mutual Respect & Community Conduct

No harassment, severe hate speech, discrimination, unsolicited advertising or excessive toxicity across in-game chat and Discord. We foster a mature, welcoming atmosphere.

2 Fair Play: Zero Hacks or Illegal Modifications

Any client modification providing unfair advantages (X-Ray, KillAura, Baritone, fly/speed hacks, auto-clickers) is strictly prohibited. Only cosmetic or client-side performance mods (Sodium, Iris, entity-free minimaps) are permitted. When joining the official server through DrakeStar-Launcher, the launcher enforces server-authoritative mod sync, blocks known cheat clients, and transparently audits SHA-256 hashes of extra client mods under our Privacy Policy.

3 Strict Prohibition of Duplication Exploits

Item duping via crash exploits, inventory desync, or void glitches is forbidden. Players discovering bugs must report them immediately to SAORI or Staff via Discord tickets. White-hat reporters receive rewards; abusers face permanent bans.

4 Protection of Land Claims & Player Property

Griefing, raiding, or bypassing claimed territory protections using pistons, fluid flow, or explosions is penalized. Respect your neighbors' creations.

5 Account Security & Official Economy

You are solely responsible for your account credentials. Real-money trading (RMT) of in-game items or currency outside the official Tebex store (drakescraft-v2.tebex.io) is strictly prohibited.
