Votes · Rewards

Vote and earn rewards

Every vote counts: it helps more players find us and gives you in-game rewards. Vote with your exact nick, once per site in each cooldown window.

Saved only in this browser. Rewards go to that nick.

Voting sites

Timer for this browser only; the server validates every vote on its own.

Rewards

Per vote

  • 150 drachmas in your wallet
  • 2 Steel Ingots (Slimefun)
  • 1 Hercules Relicary key (/oraculo)

Weekly bonus: every site

Vote on every enabled site within the same week (Monday to Sunday, Chile time) and get, once a week: 800 drachmas, 1 Hardened Metal and 1 Hermes key.

Daily streak

Vote at least once a day. Skip a day and the streak resets to 1. Your first vote each day adds 50 drachmas per streak day (cap: 7 days).

Streak dayExtra reward
3300 drachmas + 4 Silver Ingots
71,000 drachmas + 2 24K Gold + 1 Athena key
142,500 drachmas + 4 Damascus Steel + 2 Athena keys
306,000 drachmas + 2 Synthetic Diamonds + 1 Zeus key

How votes are counted

  • Each vote is paid once; duplicates are discarded.
  • Money goes to the wallet of the game mode you are in when you vote; if you are offline or in the lobby, to Survival.
  • Items and keys arrive when you log in if you were offline.
  • Votes cannot be bought or transferred.
