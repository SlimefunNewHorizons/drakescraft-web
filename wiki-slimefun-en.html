Frequently asked

Where does the data come from?

From the Slimefun registry of the running server (items, machines and recipes) plus our recipe dump. It is regenerated by a script, not typed by hand.

Why do some items have no recipe or description?

Not every item has been dumped yet. We only show what the server reports: no guesses.

How do I search?

Type part of a name, or pick an addon and then a category. Results load on demand, so it stays light on mobile.
