How Island Level Works (SkyBlock & OneBlock)

The number one community question: «I have been building for hours and my level hasn't changed». This is normal. The island level is computed on demand rather than on every block place to preserve performance. Here is the canonical breakdown:

Calculated On Demand

The island level recalculates when you explicitly execute /is level (or /ob level in OneBlock) and automatically upon logging into the server. In between calculations, rankings display the last saved snapshot.

60-Second Cooldown

There is a 1-minute cooldown between level scans. Requesting scans repeatedly will prompt a wait timer.

Placed Blocks Count, Not Mined

Level calculations audit blocks physically placed within your island territory. Breaking or storing blocks does not contribute; valuable materials must remain placed.

100 Points = 1 Island Level

The total point value of placed blocks is divided by 100. Reference values: Diamond Blocks and Beacons = 300 pts; Netherite, Gold, Emerald Blocks, Ender Chests, Enchanting Tables, and Dragon Eggs = 150 pts. In contrast, Stone, Cobblestone, Netherrack, and Logs = 0 pts (preventing filler exploit inflation).

Chest Inventories Ignored

The scanner strictly audits world blocks, ignoring contents inside chests, barrels, and shulkers.

Nether & End Do Not Count

Only the primary Overworld island structure contributes to the level calculation.

Deaths Deduct 1 Level

Each death deducts 100 points (1 full level). In team islands, only deaths by the island leader deduct points.

Starting Island Value Normalized

Starter schematic blocks are excluded so all players begin equally at level 0.

Anti-Spam Block Caps

Caps of 10,000 cobblestone and 1,000 netherrack ensure clean architectural progression over filler spam.

Village Raids on SkyBlock Islands

Raids are fully enabled in SkyBlock, but floating void environments require proper spawning foundations per vanilla Minecraft rules:

Initiating a Raid

Drink an Ominous Bottle (Bad Omen) and enter your village (villagers with claimed beds/workstations). The raid starts within 30 seconds.

Island Settings

Ensure «Natural monster spawning» and «Raid trigger» are allowed in /is settings for your rank.

Spawn Platforms Required

Minecraft searches for the highest solid blocks around the village boundary. In the void, this search drops to Y=0 and cancels if no platform exists. Build a wide, solid platform at village height (open to the sky with 3 blocks clearance) 10–40 blocks away from the village center.

How the 6 Modalities Work

All modes operate on the same unified host. You don't switch servers or IPs: switch environments seamlessly with /modalidades while Odysseia securely snapshots your inventory.

Slimefun

The primary and most expansive survival mode. Previously named Survival.

  • Full Slimefun ecosystem with 60+ addons
  • Magic systems, pantheon gods, and boss arenas
  • ProtectionStones land claim management
  • Community auctions, markets, and player warps
  • Difficulty: Hard

Classic

Pure traditional Minecraft. Just you, your tools, and the wilderness.

  • Zero Slimefun: no machines, no guides, no tech
  • Pure Vanilla Mobs: no level scaling, no infernals, no bosses
  • Terralith world generation with breathtaking biomes
  • 200,000-block world border with dedicated /rtp

SkyBlock

Your private island floating in the void. Start from scratch with minimal supplies.

  • Island territory expands as you rank up in /rankup
  • Dedicated Nether and End void dimensions
  • Slimefun fully enabled
  • Difficulty: Normal
  • Enter with /is

OneBlock

A solitary infinite block beneath your feet that continuously evolves.

  • 21 progressive stages with escalating rewards
  • From serene Plains to hazardous Nether and End stages
  • Island boundary auto-expands with /rankup
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Enter with /ob

OneChunk

Dig down through an isolated 16x16 chunk with rotating biomes.

  • Enter with /onechunk

Peaceful flat sandbox for stress-testing complex machines and networks.
