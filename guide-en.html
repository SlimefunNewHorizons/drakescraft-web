Season 2

Datapacks and Bedrock access

What each Season 2 datapack adds, and how to join from Bedrock.

Season 2: datapacks

Season 2 adds datapacks to the Slimefun worlds only. Classic stays vanilla with Terralith terrain. New worlds and unexplored chunks use the new generation; areas you already explored do not change.

Terrain

Terratonic

Rewrites the Overworld relief (density and noise functions): bolder, more varied landscapes.

  • Only affects chunks not generated yet.
  • Works alongside Terralith biomes.
Nether

Incendium

Reworks the Nether with new biomes, structures, mobs and items.

  • Biomes: Ash Barrens, Infernal Dunes, Inverted Forest, Quartz Flats, Toxic Heap, Volcanic Deltas, Weeping Valley, Withered Forest.
  • Structures: Forbidden Castle, Sanctum, Infernal Altar, Nether Reactor, Piglin Village, Quartz Kitchen, Ruined Lab, Abandoned Tower.
End

Stellarity

An End expansion with new mechanics, aimed at experienced players.

  • Altars: the Altar of the Accursed (dark magic, cursed crafting) and the Altar of the Sacred.
  • Cauldron: special recipes brewed in the cauldron.
  • Void fishing: fish in the void with its own loot table.
  • The dragon, pillars and crystals stay vanilla.
Structures

Yggdrasil Structure

Huge Norse-themed dungeons and cities spread across the world.

  • Asgard, Helheim, Vanaheim, Folkvangr, Glaosheimr, Alfheim Tree, Runic Labyrinth, Dokkalfar Ruins, Neogard and more.
  • Each has its own loot.
Powers

SVM Powers

Active powers with their own menu: pick and bind abilities to your playstyle.

  • Fire, ice, water, lightning, gravity, telekinesis, time, void, healing, invisibility, flora, snow, explosion, blood, death and more.
  • Abilities unlock as advancements.
Ambient horror

From the Fog

Unsettling atmosphere: sightings, ghost doors, sinister signs, chilled candles and ghost miners.

  • Rare structures: Mossy Pyramid, Sand Pyramid, Leafless Grove, Torch Cave.
  • Ambient horror with no grief: it is not meant to destroy your builds.
Detail

Spawn Animations

Mobs appear with an animation (for example rising from the ground) instead of popping in.

  • Does not affect players or items.

Not active yet: Enchants+ was removed (not compatible with 26.2) and More Mobs is not installed yet.

Join from Bedrock

From Bedrock 26.60 you no longer join by typing an IP and port. You join through a link (Warden/NetherNet) that we publish on Discord in #bedrock-unirse and here on the website.
