I

What you are seeing

A radial tree on a sphere: the centre is the root, each ring is a level and colour marks the community. Distant edges fade to give depth.

II

Where the data comes from

Static snapshots built from what the site already publishes: the Slimefun wiki, the plugin guide and the public repository's code. Regenerated with tools/gen_grafos.py.

III

What you will not see

Nothing private: no paths, IPs or real logs. The SAORI graph is illustrative and the terminal is synthesised from public server metrics.
