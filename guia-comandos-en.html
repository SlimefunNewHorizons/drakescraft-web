🌟 Basic Commands and Navigation

Essential commands to move around the network, check your status and request assistance. Available universally across all modalities.

/spawn

Takes you to the spawn point of the modality you're in. In SkyBlock, OneBlock and OneChunk there's no general public spawn: it sends you to your island or chunk safe point.Universal

/lobby

Returns you to the main hub (SpawnWarps), where the modality selection podiums and central welcome zone are located.Also: /hub, /lUniversal

/discord

Official link to the Discord community and linking your Minecraft account with the SAORI orchestrator to sync roles and rewards.Community and supportUniversal

/jack <message>

Report a technical bug, exploit or request direct assistance from Jack and SAORI. It's the fastest way to alert about a bug or priority ticket.Also: /reportar, /bug, /ticket, /supportUniversal

/ping

Check your real latency and response time in milliseconds with the Dallas server.Universal

/playtime

Check your total accumulated playtime on the server. It's the official statistic that weights for veteran advancement.Universal

/rules

Display official server rules and coexistence guidelines.Also: /reglas, /normasUniversal

/motd

Shows the official message of the day and recent network updates.Universal

/list

List of connected players and staff on duty.Universal

/afk

Places you in away mode to notify your companions.Universal

/help

Native system help menu.Also: /ayuda, /commandsUniversal

/graves

Panel of your graves. When you die, items don't drop to the ground: everything is saved in a protected grave with your head and experience (AxGraves). It's preserved for 7 days.List of coordinates: /axgraves list · Teleport: /axgraves tp Universal

/bienvenida [es|en]

Re-open the welcome menu with the startup guide for Slimefun, SkyBlock and OneBlock. The optional argument forces the language.Universal

/niebla [player|all]

Toggles Halloween and terror atmospheric fog. Without arguments it toggles personally; with staff permissions it can affect other players or everyone (all).Events

/crates [balance]

Opens the virtual crate menu (DrakesCrates). With balance shows your accumulated virtual keys.Global

/oraculo [reclamar]

Opens the Oracle and the Relics of Olympus. Without arguments opens the GUI; claim claims all your pending rewards.Global

🌐 Native Multilingual Translator (DrakesTranslate)

Bidirectional neural translation in real-time between Spanish, English, Portuguese and German. Requires no mods on your client and is 100% compatible with Java and Bedrock.

/translate on <language>

Enable automatic chat translation to the selected language (e.g: /translate on en for English, /translate on es for Spanish).Languages: es, en, pt, de · Alias: /tr on, /traductor onBedrock & Java

/translate off

Disable automatic translation to see messages in their original language.Alias: /tr offUniversal

/translate toggle

Quickly toggle the translator on and off with a single command.Alias: /tr toggleUniversal

/translate me <language> <message>

Translates what you write to the target language before sending it so others read it translated.Alias: /trmeUniversal

/translate status

Check your translation status, target language and neural engine status.Alias: /tr status, /tr infoUniversal

🧱 OneChunk Modality (Mine Through a Chunk)

You start in a 16x16 chunk generated for you: the first is always a forest with trees, iron, coal, stone and dirt. What you mine doesn't reappear: you mine through the chunk. Upon exhausting it (when you've mined almost everything) the next appears, and every 3 chunks the biome changes (forest, desert, snowy tundra, jungle and deep caverns). With each chunk more hostile mobs appear, increasingly dangerous, and ores become richer.

/onechunk

Takes you to your chunk. First time creates your island and generates the first chunk.Also: /ocOneChunk

/oc team

Manage your team to play together in the same chunk.OneChunk

/oc sethome

Save a return point within your island.OneChunk

Careful When Digging

If the chunk runs out while you're inside, the next appears above you: the system brings you to the surface safely (you, your pets and what you dropped). Previous chunk mobs disappear.OneChunk
