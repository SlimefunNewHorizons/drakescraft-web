⭐ Active Divine Abilities, Buffs & VIP++ GUI Menu (DrakesVIP++) New System

DrakesVIP++ introduces a full layer of active gameplay and cosmetics for all Olympian and Titan ranks. Each deity possesses a unique active divine ability, permanent extra hearts, environmental passive effects, and an interactive in-game menu (/vip) where you can toggle every perk individually to your preference with automatic cross-session persistence.

⚡ Active Ability Trigger

To unleash the power of your Deity or Titan, press Sneak (Shift) + Key F (Swap item with offhand).

  • Cooldown: 30 seconds base for Gods.
  • Titan Cronos & Chaos: Cooldown reduced by -50% (15 seconds).
  • Protected Worlds: Disabled in strict competitive balance zones and PvP arenas to preserve combat integrity.

🎮 Interactive GUI Menu (/vip)

Type /vip or /vip menu at any time to open your settings panel:

  • Toggle between ENABLED and DISABLED with a single click.
  • Control: Active Ability, Buffs & Health, Cosmetic Aura, Double Jump, Join Burst, and Kill Flourish.
  • Your preferences are permanently saved to your persistent player profile (PDC).
  • Use /vip info to inspect your text booster multipliers and status.
  • Ranks Catalog: Click the center book (slot 13) or type /vip catalogo (or /vip all) to open the full 54-slot catalog detailing all 15 VIP ranks, active abilities, passives, and multipliers.

✨ Cosmetics, Auras & Death Effects Aesthetics

Equip via /cosmeticos and remove by typing none: /cosmeticos aura none. The visual menu marks owned items in green and locked rank perks in gray.

/cosmeticos aura <nombre>

Persistent particles encircling your character. Models feature true 3D geometry: flapping wings, halos, tails, and orbits.

/cosmeticos rastro <nombre>

Footstep particle trails left behind when walking or sprinting.

/cosmeticos muerte <nombre>

Cinematic particle burst triggered upon death.

/cosmeticos aura none

Disables your active aura. Works identically for trail and death.

Cosmetics auto-revalidate: if a rank pass expires, the aura safely turns off. Designed purely for aesthetic prestige with zero combat unfairness.

⛩️ DrakesRankup System: 50 Anime Progression Ranks In-Game Economy

The DrakesRankup system is the official personal progression ladder on DrakesCraft. Unlike donor ranks obtained on the store, these ranks are purchased 100% with in-game currency (Dragmas) earned via jobs (/jobs), trading, farming, quests, and vote crates.

⚡ Full Compatibility with VIP Ranks

If you possess a Gods or Titans donor rank (Hercules, Hermes, Zeus, Chaos, etc.), your VIP benefits are never lost or downgraded.

  • Your player vaults, homes, flight permissions (/fly), and VIP kits remain intact.
  • Your anime rank adds your cosmetic badge prefix, visual effects, and tablist prestige.
  • They stack seamlessly: you can be VIP Zeus while holding the anime rank Gojo or KamiSama.

🏺 Maintenance & Rank Stability System (Resonance Core & Checkpoints) Active Economy

🌐 Supported Game Modes

El sistema de DrakesRankup (100 rangos anime, kits con ítems custom, transformaciones de Ki y Núcleo de Estabilidad) está activo exclusivamente en las modalidades custom: Survival SF (Slimefun), OneBlock (OB), SkyBlock (SKY) y OneChunk (CH).
❌ Survival Clásico se mantiene como una experiencia 100% Vanilla tradicional, sin rangos custom, transformaciones ni kits OP para garantizar un balance clásico puro.

En DrakesCraft, el poder supremo exige disciplina y constancia. Los rangos intermedios y de trascendencia superior cuentan con un Núcleo de Resonancia de Ki con una duración base de 14 días (2 semanas). Si un rango temporal pasa 14 días sin mantención, decae suavemente hacia el último Checkpoint permanente de su división.

🛡️ Rangos Permanentes y Checkpoints Sagrados

  • División I Completa (Tiers 1 al 10): Es 100% Permanente. Los novatos y aprendices nunca pierden su nivel.
  • Division Milestones (Tiers 10, 20, 30, 40): Act as Sacred Anchors that never decay.
  • Tier 50 (King of the Pirates / KamiSama): The eternal zenith, 100% Permanent.
  • Transient Tiers: Tiers 11-19, 21-29, 31-39, and 41-49. If stability expires, you never fall to 0: you simply roll back to your division anchor (e.g., from Tier 28 to Tier 20).

💰 How Maintenance Works

  • Free Upon Rankup!: Every time you advance with /rankup or /rankup max, your stability timer automatically resets to 14 days free of charge.
  • Renewal Tribute: If you decide to remain at a rank, renew 14 days paying only 5% of rank cost (min. 5,000 Dragmas).
  • From GUI: Open /rankup and click Slot 45 (🏺 Stability Core).
  • Direct Command: Type /rankup maintain in chat.
  • Preventative Alerts: The server notifies you with musical chimes and chat alerts when under 72 hours remain.
