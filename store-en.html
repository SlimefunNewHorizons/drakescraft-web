WE ANSWER YOUR QUESTIONS

Store Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about deliveries, platforms, passes and security before acquiring your upgrade.

How and when is my purchase delivered in-game?

Delivery is 100% automated via Tebex and the Odysseia system. As soon as your payment is confirmed, ranks, kits or Dragmas are applied to the server within 1 to 5 minutes. If you're connected you'll receive effects and items live; if you're offline they'll be credited automatically when you join.

How long do "permanent" passes (like Anti-AFK) and ranks last?

Per DrakesCraft's canonical rules, items labeled as "permanent" (such as the Permanent Anti-AFK Pass, extra vaults and homes, Premium Economy and Slimefun Knowledge; Tebex may label them "permanent" or "lifetime", but nothing is for life) remain active for the entire duration of the active season in which they were purchased, concluding when that season reaches its official wipe/conclusion. Passes from Season 1 expired with Season 1, and passes acquired in Season 2 remain valid throughout all of Season 2.

Do monthly ranks auto-renew?

No, absolutely not. DrakesCraft has no hidden subscriptions or surprise card charges. Each monthly rank (Thor, Anubis, Poseidon, etc.) is a single 30-day pass. When it expires, you decide freely whether to buy another one or keep your account free.

Can I buy if I play from Bedrock (mobile, Win10 or consoles)?

Yes, fully compatible. In the platform selector above, select Bedrock. The store will automatically add the required leading dot (for example, .YourNick) so Geyser integration delivers your purchases exactly to your Bedrock account.

Do my ranks and benefits work in all game modes?

Your rank and your Dragmas go with you, but inventories and auctions are independent per game mode, and the Lab is creative-only with no trading (see the guide). Per-mode limits for vaults, homes and protections are not itemized: if it matters for a specific mode, ask on Discord before buying.

Does the /fly command from Hermes and Titans have a limit or consume energy?

It consumes no energy, fuel, batteries or in-game money. The /fly command granted by ranks and kits is free flight in your protected regions and in areas where it is enabled, for as long as your pass lasts (30 days for ranks, Titans included), in your protected regions and in areas where it is enabled.

What if I got my username wrong at checkout?

Don't worry, you won't lose your purchase. Every transaction generates an official Tebex order number sent to your email. Go to our Official Discord, open a ticket in #support with your receipt and staff will transfer your package to your correct account at no additional cost.

Why are prices in US dollars?

Tebex charges in US dollars (USD). The conversion to other currencies shown in the store is only an estimate from public rates: the real charge is in USD and your bank or Tebex sets the exchange rate. Tebex is the Merchant of Record and may apply taxes depending on your country.
