Season 2 · Olympus in Collapse

Olympus can no
longer hold

The sky of the gods has split. A Greek island floats over the void, the Rift tears open its side, and the End climbs through its wounds.

STATUS S2 in testing · Staff onlyREOPENING Planned for November 18 · to be confirmed on Discord

No one remembers what broke first. Some say the moon. Others say Olympus had been floating on a lie for centuries, and it only took someone looking down.

What is certain is this: the island of the gods is still in the air, but it no longer belongs to them. The End climbs through its wounds, marble turns purple, and above the fountain where mortals choose their fate hangs a machine no one admits to building.

Season 2 starts here. Not in some distant world: in the first place you set foot.

Canto I

The Rift

It split the southeast meadow into three branches and kept climbing to the foot of the great temple. The temple's corner has already given way, bursting toward the abyss, and through the gap you can see what lies below.

End spires sprout from its edges. Around it, the ground rots. The clouds tell it best: divine gold to the north, purple to the south.

In the lobby: follow its edge from the meadow up to the great temple.

Canto II

Atlas won't let go

They condemned him to hold up the sky. When the sky broke, no one came to tell him he could stop.

He is still there above the south meadow, golden cuirass, purple cape in tatters, carrying a shattered End moon on his shoulders. If he gives way, it falls on everything that is left.

In the lobby: see him from the great temple, moon overhead.

Canto III

The Antikythera

Gold rings, broken teeth, gears that no longer mesh. It once predicted eclipses and games; now it hangs above the fountain like a clock refusing to admit the hour.

Right below it, travelers pick their gamemode without looking up. Perhaps that is for the best.

In the lobby: it hangs above the fountain, over the NPCs.

Canto IV

Something is listening

Nothing on Olympus should have antennas. And yet over the east meadow rises a wall of black and gold lattice, like the Duga radar, flanked by dishes aimed at nothing.

A golden circuit runs along the paths over black stone, fed by pylons. Someone turned the sacred mountain into a listening post. We don't know what it heard.

In the lobby: the antenna wall towers over the east meadow.

Canto V

What answered

They say a world that screams ends up being heard. The Wither Storm is no legend this season: it devours the careless, and it grows.

The gods don't mention it. Those who have gone down to the worlds do.

In S2: the Wither Storm datapack is active on the server.

Canto VI

The Colosseum stays open

The sky is falling and they are still fighting. The colosseum lost its crown, but the arena and the stands remain intact.

When the gods no longer judge, only PvP is left to settle who was right.

In the lobby: the PvP colosseum lies to the east, crown broken.

Canto VII

What broke away

Chunks of island drift off dragging ruins and roots. The mortals, practical as ever, moved in anyway.

Four of them serve as stops: Protections, Crates, Vote and Store. Below them all, and below Olympus itself, hangs the inverted mountain they were torn from.

In the lobby: each island has its own warp.

TECHNICAL CANON · DALLAS PRODUCTION

Technical Dossier: Season 2 Hybrid Engine

To sustain 202 plugins without degrading TPS or suffering Garbage Collector stalls, DrakesCraft S2 bridges Java extensibility with Rust performance through Java 25 Project Panama (FFM).

01 // HYBRID ARCHITECTURE

Java 25 + Rust FFM ("Street Car with F1 Turbo")

Java Chassis: DrakesPurpur 26.2 running on OpenJDK 64-Bit 25 LTS. All 202 Bukkit/Paper plugins operate transparently while 3 native Rust libraries run beneath: libdrakes_hybrid_ffi.so, libslimefun_ffi.so, and libodysseia_ffi.so.

Project Panama (FFM): Allows Java and Rust to share raw RAM in nanoseconds without slow JNI bridges or Garbage Collector memory churn. Rust executes chunk bit packing (4.32x faster in 4096x8 benchmarks), 3D Perlin noise terrain math, .mca region parsing, Slimefun energy grids, and Redstone clock monitoring.

Zero-Downtime Fallback: If a native library is missing, the engine gracefully falls back to Java without server crash or player disconnects.
02 // WORLDS & SPEED

The Bullet Train (Predictive Prefetch + LOD)

Predictive Flight Radar: Flying with Elytra or /fly no longer collides with unloaded voids. The radar projects a flight cone 3.5 seconds ahead, dispatching 6 Moonrise threads and 4 Rust workers to load and generate chunks before arrival.

Ticket Retention & LOD: Each chunk is locked with a 6-second ticket preventing premature cache evictions, integrated with Distant Horizons-style LOD.

The Monolith server.jar: All 9 Drakes-Suites (Core, Tech, Magic, Bio, Combat, Generators, Utility, Server & Multiverse) are shipped bundled inside the jar, verified via SHA-256 (drakes-data/suites.yml). Protected with enforced world saves and InventoryRollbackPlus.

03 // SMART CLIENT

DrakeStar-Launcher ("Steam for Mods")

Delta Sync (modsync.rs): Server command /drakes hybrid manifest generates client-manifest.json with SHA-256 hashes. The launcher diffs mods in milliseconds: modifying 1 mod of 5 MB in a 150-mod pack only downloads that single 5 MB file.

Rust Anti-Explosion Scanner: Under 50 ms before launch, the launcher audits fabric.mod.json and mixin descriptors, blocking missing dependencies, duplicate jars, explicit conflicts, and bytecode collisions (MixinTargetOverlap).

Zero Surprise Crashes: Guarantees clean game launches without mysterious Mojang loading screen freezes.

Fair Play, Equality & Field Calibration

Season 2 is a massive leap with six connected modalities, enhanced Slimefun mechanics, advanced logistics, and divine powers. By design, we enforce a strict zero-risk policy in production until the planned launch on November 18, 2026.

In a project of this scale, it is impossible to anticipate every single edge case in advance. You, our players, will be our most valuable asset through the Discord ticket system. Community feedback will help us calibrate economy flows, prevent currency or enchantment exploits, and fine-tune abilities in real time.

Our Commitment: A fair, balanced environment where every player starts on equal footing to build their own legacy.

Seasonal Validity & Reset Policy

Per DrakesCraft regulations, each season starts a fresh cycle of civilization and progression. Passes and ranks labeled as "permanent" (specifically including the Permanent Anti-AFK Pass) carry guaranteed validity for the entire active season in which they were purchased. Passes purchased during Season 1 remained valid until the end of Season 1; likewise, permanent passes purchased during Season 2 are valid throughout the entirety of Season 2.
