Possession of a VIP rank or sponsorship does not grant immunity nor relaxation of rule compliance:

  • Litematica as Visual Guide (Holograms): Using Litematica in hologram/visual guide mode is 100% legal and permitted in all game modes. You can load schematics to see ghost blocks and build with visual guidance.

  • Sanction Evasion: Using alternate accounts, borrowed accounts, VPNs, or proxies to access the server or Discord while your main account is sanctioned converts the sanction to a PERMANENT AND UNAPPEALABLE BAN for all associated accounts and IP block.
