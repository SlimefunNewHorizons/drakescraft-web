What to include in your report:
  • Game mode where it happened (Slimefun, Classic, SkyBlock, etc.).
  • Coordinates of the affected block or machine (X, Y, Z).
  • Screenshot or short clip of the issue.
  • Simple steps to reproduce the issue.

In-Game Support Commands

Ask for help or get answers directly from the chat in any game mode.

/helpop <mensaje>

Sends an urgent alert with a sound notification to all online moderators.

/jack <mensaje>

Notifies Jack (Owner & Architect) about critical server or kernel issues.

/discord

Posts the official invite link in chat, synchronized with your account and ranks.

/ping

Measures your latency to the Dallas node to help identify local connection issues.

⚠️

Strict Security Protocol (Zero Trust)

NEVER share your passwords, card numbers, access keys, or authentication codes with anyone. No DrakesCraft staff member, Saori, or Jack will ever ask for your /login password or private banking details. Legitimate purchase checks use only your Minecraft username and official Tebex transaction ID.
